# Phase 2 QA Acceptance Test Report (`p2-1`)

## Summary

Implemented the deterministic Phase 2 acceptance test suite under `tests/backend/` and shared Forge SDK test doubles under `tests/helpers/`, enforcing every requirement (`R1`–`R6`, `R8`–`R11`, `R14`) and contract invariant in `shared/phase-2/design.md` and `shared/phase-2/contract.md`.

### Added / Updated Test Files

- `tests/helpers/domain-errors.ts` — Added `expectAsyncPokerDomainError` alongside `expectPokerDomainError` so async domain error assertions verify `PokerDomainError` subclass identity and exact `PokerErrorCode` (preventing false positives against unimplemented stubs).
- `tests/helpers/forge-doubles.ts` — Added deterministic in-memory `@forge/kvs` double (tracking `KVS_TTL_24H` options, ordered KVS operations, and live offset-based `nextCursor` pagination), Jira REST v3 router double (`GET /rest/api/3/myself`, `GET /rest/api/3/issue/{issueId}/editmeta`, `PUT /rest/api/3/issue/{issueId}`), `@forge/realtime` event recorder with fault injection, and structured `LogSink` capture double.
- `tests/backend/observability-and-manifest.test.ts` — Acceptance tests for `src/observability/logger.ts` and `manifest.yml` compliance (`R14`).
- `tests/backend/session-store.test.ts` — Acceptance tests for `src/storage/session-store.ts` (`R2`, `R3`, `R4`, `R9`).
- `tests/backend/jira-and-realtime.test.ts` — Acceptance tests for `src/jira/jira-client.ts` and `src/realtime/publisher.ts` (`R5`, `R10`, `R11`, `R14`).
- `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts` — Acceptance tests across all 7 Forge RPC resolver handlers in `src/resolvers/poker-resolvers.ts` (`R1`–`R6`, `R8`–`R11`, `R14`).
- `tests/backend/forge-sdk-wiring.test.ts` — Acceptance tests for default `@forge/kvs`, `@forge/api` (`api.asUser()` vs forbidden `api.asApp()`), `@forge/realtime`, and `src/index.ts` `handler` registration (`R2`, `R10`, `R11`, `R14`).

## Coverage Mapped to `## Test outline`

- **R1 (`unit` — `castVote` deck & payload validation):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts`: verifies `castVote` accepts all 9 canonical deck cards (`'0'`, `'1'`, `'2'`, `'3'`, `'5'`, `'8'`, `'13'`, `'21'`, `'?'`) and null-prototype plain objects, and rejects empty strings, whitespace-padded strings (`' 5'`, `'5 '`, `' 5 '`), negative strings (`'-1'`), decimals (`'3.5'`), non-Fibonacci integers (`'4'`, `'6'`, `'34'`), arbitrary words (`'coffee'`), `null`, `undefined`, numeric primitives, arrays, `Date`, class instances, and missing payloads with `PokerDomainError` `code: 'validation-error'` before performing any KVS, Jira, or realtime calls.
- **R1 (`unit` — `selectOverrideScore` & `applyStoryPoints` score & payload validation):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts`: verifies `selectOverrideScore` and `applyStoryPoints` accept all 8 numeric Fibonacci scores (`0`, `1`, `2`, `3`, `5`, `8`, `13`, `21`) and reject `-0`, `-1`, `4`, `4.5`, `22`, `NaN`, `Infinity`, `-Infinity`, `'?'`, numeric strings, `null`, `undefined`, and missing/non-plain-object payloads with `PokerDomainError` `code: 'validation-error'`, leaving prior `'revealed'` session state intact and making zero KVS or Jira calls.
- **R2 (`unit` — Host context isolation, spoofing defense, idle snapshot & identifier validation):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/session-store.test.ts`, `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts`, and `tests/backend/forge-sdk-wiring.test.ts`: verifies `assertSafeIdentifier`, `extractAuthenticatedContext`, and all 7 resolver handlers scope KVS keys and Jira calls strictly to `context.extension.issue.id` and `context.accountId` (including colon-delimited Atlassian account IDs), ignore spoofed `issueId`/`issueKey`/`accountId` fields in `payload` across `Issue A` vs `Issue B`, return an idle `'voting'` snapshot with `0` participants and `null` card/score fields on uninitialized issues, and reject missing, blank, whitespace-padded, overlong (`129`-char), or path-separator (`/`, `\`, `\x00`) identifiers with `PokerDomainError` `code: 'validation-error'` and zero KVS or Jira I/O.
- **R3 (`unit` — `castVote` & `withdrawVote` lifecycle, active voter rule & `'revealed'` guard):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts` and `tests/backend/session-store.test.ts`: verifies recording an initial vote (`1` participant, `viewerCard` set, `KVS_TTL_24H`), updating `'5' -> '8'` (`1` participant), removing caller's vote via `withdrawVote` and via same-card toggle in `castVote` (`0` participants, `viewerCard: null`, zero `getCurrentUserProfile` calls on toggle-off), treating stored voter records as active iff `!isRoundExpired(vote.votedAt, nowMs)` (overwriting an expired same-card record in `castVote` rather than toggling off, and treating absent/expired voter records or idle rounds in `withdrawVote` idempotently without mutating `meta` or peers' votes), and rejecting `castVote` and `withdrawVote` on active `'revealed'` rounds with `PokerDomainError` `code: 'invalid-state'` and zero Jira calls.
- **R4 (`unit` — Concurrent `Promise.all` isolation & `>100`-voter cursor pagination):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts` and `tests/backend/session-store.test.ts`: verifies concurrent `Promise.all` votes across 5 distinct participants on active `'voting'`, idle (`null -> 'round-1'`), and expired (`'round-4' -> 'round-5'`) rounds via deterministic `nextRoundId` (including `BigInt` counter safety), verifies concurrent peer update (`'3' -> '5'`) + withdrawal + new vote (`'13'`) with zero lost updates, and verifies multi-page KVS `nextCursor` pagination across `125` participants returns 100% of active voters on `getSession`/`revealVotes` and deletes 100% of prior-round keys on `resetRound`/`applyStoryPoints` without mid-scan cursor truncation.
- **R5 (`unit` — Voter-only projection, ordering, `'Unknown User'` fallback, vote secrecy & realtime redaction):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts` and `tests/backend/jira-and-realtime.test.ts`: verifies snapshots include only active voters ordered by ascending `votedAt` then ascending lexicographical `accountId`, normalize blank/whitespace or failed `/rest/api/3/myself` profiles to `'Unknown User'`, strip `emailAddress`, redact all `participants[i].card` values to `null` during `'voting'` while exposing `viewerCard` solely to the requesting voter, and omit all card values and PII from `@forge/realtime` `'scrum-poker'` payloads.
- **R6 (`unit` — Democratic `revealVotes`, `0`-vote/idle/reset rejection & idempotent repeat reveal):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts`: verifies any authenticated viewer (including non-voting observers) transitions a `'voting'` round with `>= 1` active vote to `'revealed'` (exposing cards, computing scores, setting `targetScore` to `recommendedScore`, refreshing `updatedAt`, publishing `SESSION_UPDATED`), rejects `revealVotes` on `0`-vote, idle, reset, or all-votes-expired rounds with `PokerDomainError` `code: 'precondition-failed'` and zero Jira calls, and handles repeat/concurrent `revealVotes` on an active `'revealed'` round idempotently while preserving any `targetScore` override with zero extra KVS writes.
- **R8 (`unit` — Post-reveal `selectOverrideScore`):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts`: verifies any viewer on an active `'revealed'` round can set shared `targetScore` (`0..21`, including on all-`'?'` rounds where `average` and `recommendedScore` remain `null`) while preserving recorded votes and publishing `SESSION_UPDATED`, and rejects `selectOverrideScore` on `'voting'`, idle, or expired rounds with `PokerDomainError` `code: 'invalid-state'` and zero Jira calls.
- **R9 (`unit` — `resetRound` & exact 24-hour expiration boundary):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts` and `tests/backend/session-store.test.ts`: verifies `resetRound` writes a new `roundId` in `'voting'` state with `0` participants before deleting prior-round votes and publishes `SESSION_UPDATED`; verifies rounds and individual `votedAt` records remain active at `elapsed = 86_399_999` ms and negative clock skew (`nowMs < updatedAt`) and expire at `>= 86_400_000` ms (`getSession` returns idle `'voting'` without extending `updatedAt`, `castVote` starts a fresh round discarding stale votes, `revealVotes` rejects with `'precondition-failed'`, and `selectOverrideScore`/`applyStoryPoints` reject with `'invalid-state'`).
- **R10 (`unit` — `getEditableStoryPointsField` & cross-handler `storyPointsField` resolution):**
  - Covered in `tests/backend/jira-and-realtime.test.ts`, `tests/backend/poker-resolvers.test.ts`, and `tests/backend/forge-sdk-wiring.test.ts`: verifies `GET /rest/api/3/issue/{issueId}/editmeta` via `api.asUser()`, `'Story Points'` over `'Story point estimate'` precedence and lexicographical `fieldId` tie-breaking, verifies all 6 `SessionSnapshot`-returning handlers (`getSession`, `castVote`, `withdrawVote`, `revealVotes`, `selectOverrideScore`, `resetRound`) populate `snapshot.storyPointsField` once guards pass (making zero Jira calls when any guard rejects), and returns `null` cleanly on `401`/`403` (silently), `404`/`500`, malformed JSON, transport errors, or non-matching fields.
